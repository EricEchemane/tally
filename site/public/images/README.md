# Images the landing page still needs

Each slot on the page shows a dashed placeholder with its file name. Save the image here under that name and the
placeholder gets swapped for it. Use photos you have the rights to: your own shoot, a licensed stock photo, or a
generated image.

**The brief for every photo:** a happy, confident Filipino accountant whose day just got easier because of Tally.
She is promoting the product: smiling, relaxed, finished early, phone in hand. No stress, no piles of chaos in
focus.

**Rules that keep the photos usable**
- **Brand colours:** deep green-teal (`#0f2b26`) and lime (`#c5f06a`). Dress her in green, teal, white or cream, and keep a clean, bright office with plants. No blue or purple casts.
- **No readable text on screens or paper.** AI image tools garble it, and we never show a fake app. Keep the laptop and phone screens blank, dark or turned slightly away; the page draws the real Tally UI beside the photo.
- **Leave breathing room** on the side named below, so the page can place badges and buttons there.
- **No real receipts, brands or company names** in frame.

- **Format:** landscape 4:3, at least 1600 × 1200. Leave space on the right.
- **Story:** she used to check every receipt by eye; now she snaps them with her phone and she's done.

She looks relieved and confident, as if her work just got easy. Wearing a soft sage-green blouse, natural window daylight, green plants in the background, fresh green and cream colour palette, shallow depth of field, professional commercial photography, realistic, blank screens, no readable text, no logos, 4:3, empty space on the right.
- **Stock search terms:** "happy Asian accountant smartphone receipt", "smiling Filipino professional office phone", "cheerful businesswoman photographing receipt".
