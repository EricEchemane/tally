# Team email and privacy notice

Type: task (HITL)
Status: open
Blocked by: 03, 06

## Question

Set up the shared team email and write the Privacy notice page.

- HITL: the user creates the team email address (for example a Gmail made for the team).
- The notice names the team under "Tally PH" as the one collecting the data, says what Early Access Requests and Tester feedback contain, why, where they are stored (from Form storage and email service), how long they are kept, and how to ask for deletion, as the Data Privacy Act requires.
- It also says what the site does not collect: no cookies and no receipts (the interactive demo uses bundled samples).
